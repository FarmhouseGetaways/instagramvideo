# 14-Day Viral Plan: Oct 1–14, 2026

**Goal:** daily videos for all 3 accounts, aiming for 1M+ total views in 2 weeks.
**Mix:** a Story set every day (dominant) plus a Reel or Trial Reel cut from the same footage.
**How the numbers get there:** Stories reach existing followers. The million comes from
Reels/Trial Reels reaching non-followers. So every Reel is built for **sends**
(a "send this to…" or "tag who…" line) and **rewatches** (short and loopable).
Hook A/B tests run as Trial Reels; the winning hook gets reposted as a regular Reel.

Trend sources: `knowledge/trends-2026-09.md`. Audio defaults to **Business-safe
original-audio sounds** until each account's type is confirmed. Check every sound
in the app from the posting account before using it.

## Filming (the only part Carissa does)
Two batch shoots per account, about 45 minutes each, one per week (Sep 30 and Oct 7).
Film everything **vertical, 4K 30fps, HDR off**, 3–6s per shot, steady, window
light where possible. Upload the raw clips and I handle the rest.

## Daily routine once footage is in
1. I cut the Reel (plus 2 hook variants for Trial days) and the Story set from the same clips.
2. I deliver: the videos, cover frames, a caption (hook and keywords in the first 2 lines, 3–5 hashtags),
   the sound to add in-app and its start point, and the stickers to add.
3. Post Reels during each account's peak Insights hour. Post Stories in 2–3 drops through the day.
4. At 24h and 48h, log the numbers in `tracking/results.csv`. Winners get a new hook and a repost;
   formats that flop get dropped.

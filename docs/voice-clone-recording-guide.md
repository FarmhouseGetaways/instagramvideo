# Recording Your Voice for Cloning

**Goal:** one clean 60–90 second voice memo. I use it to build a clone of your voice for narration.

## How to record (iPhone Voice Memos)
1. **Quiet room** with soft surfaces (a bedroom or closet is ideal). No fans, TV, music or kitchen noise.
2. **Phone 6–10 inches from your mouth**, slightly off to the side so your breath doesn't pop.
3. Speak the way you do on Instagram: **warm, natural, a little upbeat**. Don't "announce".
4. Leave 2 seconds of silence at the start.
5. Send the **original memo** (Share → the Claude app). Don't record a screen or a speaker.

## Read this (then add 20–30 seconds of just talking)
> Hi! Welcome to the little red barn. I'm so glad you're here.
> Every morning feels a little different — some days are quiet and slow, and some days are
> busy, loud, and so much fun.
> Have you ever found something you didn't know you needed until you saw it? That's my favorite feeling.
> Grab a coffee, take your time, and tell me — what would you pick first?
> Honestly? I love this. The early mornings, the people, all of it.

**Then just talk for 20–30 seconds** about anything: your day, why you started, what you love.
Natural speech teaches the clone your real rhythm.

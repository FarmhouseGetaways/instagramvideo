# Algorithm & Best Practices

_Last checked: 2026-09-27._

## What the Reels algorithm rewards (in order)
1. **DM shares ("sends")**: the strongest signal for reaching non-followers.
   Make content people want to send to a friend ("this is so us", "we need to
   book this", "you'd love this shop").
2. **Watch time / completion rate**: the 3-second mark is the first test.
   Loops that make people rewatch count heavily.
3. **Likes per reach**: engagement relative to how many people saw it.
4. **Originality**: reposted or watermarked (TikTok logo) content is demoted.
   Always upload clean files.
5. **Account authority**: consistent posting within one topic.

Instagram runs separate ranking systems for Feed, Stories, Reels, and Explore.

## Trial Reels
New Reels can be shown to non-followers first. If they perform well, they're
shared with followers too. Use them to A/B test hooks, 4–7 per week. They can
be scheduled (since Feb 2026). Claude can export 2–3 hook variants for this.

## Editing checklist (every video)
- [ ] Hook in the first 1–2 seconds: motion, a bold on-screen line, or a payoff shown first
- [ ] Text readable in about 1.5s per line, inside the safe zone
- [ ] Captions burned in (most people watch muted at first)
- [ ] Cut every 1–3s unless a slow, cozy mood is the point
- [ ] Cuts synced to the beat of the chosen audio
- [ ] Clear reason to send or save (tip, place to book, "tag someone")
- [ ] Ending that loops back into the start, or a clear CTA
- [ ] No watermarks from other apps

## Posting
- Caption: first line is a second hook; keywords matter (Instagram search is keyword-driven).
- Hashtags: 3–5 relevant ones, not 30.
- Share to Stories after posting; reply to comments in the first hour.
- Best times: use each account's Insights → "Most active times". Record
  findings in that account's `brand.md`.

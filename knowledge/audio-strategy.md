# Audio Strategy

_Last checked: 2026-09-27._

## Why audio matters
Trending audio gives a Reel an extra discovery path: its audio page, and a
boost while the sound is rising. Audio also sets the pacing, since cuts land on beats.

## Two rules that shape every edit
1. **Add trending audio inside the Instagram app, not in the exported file.**
   A song baked into the file isn't linked to Instagram's audio page (so it
   gets no trend boost) and may be muted for copyright. Claude edits the cuts to
   the song's beat, delivers the video **without music** (or with Carissa's
   original voice/ambient sound only), and says which song to add in-app,
   at which start point.
2. **Business accounts get a smaller music library.** They only get the Meta
   Sound Collection (~14k commercially cleared tracks). Creator/Personal
   accounts get the full library. Using major-label music in business/promotional
   content is a copyright risk. Check which account type each handle uses
   (see each `brand.md`).

## Picking audio
- Rising sounds (usually shown with an ↗ arrow in the Reels audio browser) beat
  sounds that have already peaked.
- The mood has to fit the brand. A cozy farmhouse getaway ≠ an aggressive
  hype track.
- Offer 2–3 options when mood is ambiguous: e.g., one trending, one cozy and
  evergreen, one voiceover-friendly (quiet background).
- Claude can't browse Instagram's in-app trending list. Carissa can screenshot
  Reels → camera → audio → "Trending" from the app and share it. That makes
  Claude's picks far more accurate.
